    About the map

    Every fish is one of the top 100 wallets holding the selected token. Its size is set by the USD value of its holdings, using thresholds tuned per token — a $20K wallet is a whale in a small memecoin but a minnow in a large one.

    Liquidity pools, burn addresses, exchange hot wallets and known team vesting contracts are filtered out so the biggest fish is always a real holder.

    Holders 101 and beyond are shown as schools of small background fish. Click any fish for its wallet card; hover to see its share of supply.

    Prototype: all wallets, balances and events on this page are generated. Real data lands next.
